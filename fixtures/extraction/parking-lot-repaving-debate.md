Most of the meeting went on whether the parking lot needs repaving or only crack sealing. Marcus
thinks the cracks along the west row have been there since before Pinecrest Regional Office took
the lease. Priya prefers sealing now and repaving in a few years, because a full repave would close
the lot for a week. Dana is not convinced that sealing lasts through a wet winter.

The cooperative's facilities budget for the year is already committed, so neither option is
available before the next cycle, and nobody left with anything to do.
